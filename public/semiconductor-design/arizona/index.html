Arizona's Semiconductor Landscape

Arizona is now the undisputed capital of American leading-edge chipmaking. TSMC's Fab 21 campus in north Phoenix — a $165 billion GigaFab plan — has Phase 1 (4nm) in high-volume production, Phase 2 (3nm) completing equipment installation with production targeted for 2027, and Phase 3 (2nm) under construction. Intel's Ocotillo campus in Chandler runs multiple fabs including Fab 42, and Amkor is building a $7 billion advanced-packaging campus in Peoria to close the packaging loop onshore.

The supplier gravity is immense: on-site industrial-gas plants from Linde and Air Liquide, Applied Materials and ASM support operations, and a CHIPS Act pipeline that has directed billions to TSMC's Phoenix projects. Arizona's fab cluster is the single largest concentration of cleanroom MEP demand in the Western Hemisphere — and every phase of it is schedule-driven, because tool-install windows at this scale are measured in billions of dollars of idle capital.

Fab Utilities: Power, Water & Gas in Arizona

Arizona Public Service and Salt River Project anchor fab power supply, with TSMC's campus drawing power at a scale that reshaped Valley grid planning. Water is the binding constraint: fabs negotiate directly with Phoenix and Chandler for reclaimed-water and long-term supply, and every new fab phase must demonstrate water-reclaim and recycling rates above 90% to clear municipal review. Palo Verde nuclear generation gives the Valley a carbon-light baseload story that matters for corporate ESG reporting.

Incentives & Site Selection in Arizona

The Arizona Commerce Authority's Arizona Competes Fund and the Qualified Facility tax credit (up to $20,000 per job for qualifying manufacturing) have underwritten the fab boom, alongside Foreign Trade Zone status that defers duties on imported tooling. The state's deal-closing fund has repeatedly delivered nine-figure packages, and expedited state permitting keeps environmental review on a fab-compatible clock.

Workforce Pipeline

Arizona State University's Ira A. Fulton Schools of Engineering — the nation's largest engineering school by enrollment — runs dedicated microelectronics curricula and the Southwest's deepest fab-talent pipeline, including the ASU-Macross partnership tracks. Maricopa County community colleges operate semiconductor-technician programs built with TSMC and Intel input, producing cleanroom-ready operators at scale.

Energy Code & Compliance

Arizona has no statewide commercial energy code mandate; Phoenix and Chandler enforce the IECC with local amendments. For fabs, process loads dwarf envelope loads, but Arizona's extreme cooling design days make chiller-plant and heat-rejection sizing the critical MEP discipline — and the reason local cleanroom experience matters.

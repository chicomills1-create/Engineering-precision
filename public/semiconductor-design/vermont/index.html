Vermont's Semiconductor Landscape

Vermont is a CHIPS Act fab state: GlobalFoundries' Essex Junction fab — part of GF's $16 billion US expansion with $1.5 billion in CHIPS support — is being modernized into one of the world's leading gallium-nitride (GaN) manufacturing facilities, with a TSMC-licensed GaN process targeting volume production. The state's precision-manufacturing heritage (machine tools, aerospace components) provides the workforce.

UVM's engineering programs and the state's small-but-capable advanced-manufacturing base support the GF ecosystem. For GaN, power-device, or specialty-semiconductor manufacturing, Vermont offers something unique: a CHIPS-funded fab in a state where every project gets personal attention.

Fab Utilities: Power, Water & Gas in Vermont

Green Mountain Power and Vermont Electric Cooperative serve industrials with New England rates — high, but the grid is among the greenest in America (hydro and nuclear), which matters for corporate ESG reporting. Water is pristine and abundant.

Incentives & Site Selection in Vermont

Vermont's Employment Growth Incentive (VEGI), the state's manufacturing machinery tax exemptions, and the Vermont Economic Development Authority's financing support the GF expansion. The state's small size means incentive decisions are personal and fast.

Workforce Pipeline

UVM, Vermont Tech, and Norwich engineering programs, plus the community-college advanced-manufacturing tracks, supply talent. GF's Essex Junction workforce is the state's premier cleanroom talent pool.

Energy Code & Compliance

Vermont enforces the IECC 2021-based energy code (Vermont Commercial Building Energy Standards) — aggressive. Cold-climate heat-recovery makes cleanroom compliance economical.

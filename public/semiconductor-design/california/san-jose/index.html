How does San Jose fit into California's semiconductor ecosystem?

What California does build: specialty fabs and R&D lines. Rigetti's Fab-1 in Fremont runs superconducting quantum chips; legacy fabs in the Bay Area and Southern California serve aerospace, defense, and analog markets. The state's real fab-adjacent growth is in advanced packaging R&D, photonics (with the AIM Photonics corridor), and the CHIPS-funded R&D ecosystem around Berkeley and Stanford. California's industrial power rates are the nation's highest — often 2-3x Texas or Arizona — which makes energy modeling and heat-recovery design disproportionately valuable for cleanroom operations.

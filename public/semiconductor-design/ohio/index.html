Ohio's Semiconductor Landscape

Ohio is building the 'Silicon Heartland': Intel's Ohio One campus in New Albany (Licking County) — a $20+ billion, 1,000-acre development planned for up to eight fabs — is the largest single fab investment in the Midwest, with Mod 1 targeting completion around 2030. Intel has invested over $5 billion in the site to date, with 9+ million construction hours logged and the build now in its structural/MEP phase.

The supplier gravity is already reshaping central Ohio: industrial-gas, chemical, and equipment-support operations are committing to the region, and JobsOhio's deal-closing apparatus is proven at Intel scale. For fab suppliers, test-and-assembly, or advanced-packaging evaluation, the Columbus metro is now a first-tier site-selection market — with Honda's EV battery corridor (Fayette County) adding power-electronics depth.

Fab Utilities: Power, Water & Gas in Ohio

Ohio's industrial rates are Midwest-competitive, and the state's grid reliability supports 24/7 manufacturing. Water from the region's systems requires planning at fab scale, and Intel's water-reclaim commitments set the standard.

Incentives & Site Selection in Ohio

JobsOhio — the nation's only privately-funded economic development agency — plus the state's $600 million in grants to Intel and local property-tax abatements delivered the deal. Ohio's incentive apparatus is proven at the largest scale in American fab history.

Workforce Pipeline

Ohio State's engineering programs, Columbus State's semiconductor-technician tracks (built with Intel input), and the union construction trades — 8,000-10,000 craft workers at peak Intel-related demand — form a deep bench. The state's community-college system is scaling fab-technician training aggressively.

Energy Code & Compliance

Ohio enforces the IECC 2018-based Ohio Building Code energy provisions — moderate compliance burden. Cold-winter heat-recovery design pays back for cleanroom operations.

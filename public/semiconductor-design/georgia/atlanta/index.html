Georgia's semiconductor story runs through power electronics and advanced manufacturing. The state's EV and battery corridor — Hyundai's Metaplant in Bryan County, Rivian's planned Georgia plant, SK Battery America in Commerce — has created enormous demand for power-electronics assembly, battery-management systems, and the controlled-environment production halls that build them. Georgia Tech's nanofabrication and microelectronics research adds R&D cleanroom depth.

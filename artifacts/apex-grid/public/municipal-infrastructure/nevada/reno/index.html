Municipal Infrastructure Engineering in Reno, Nevada

By Jeremy Mills, CEO & Founder, Apex Grid Engineering — USAF Veteran. · Updated 2026-10-02

Apex Grid Engineering designs municipal water, wastewater, bridge, and stormwater infrastructure in Reno, Nevada — treatment-plant upgrades, lift stations, drinking water distribution, municipal bridges, and flood-control systems. Water and wastewater service in Reno runs through Truckee Meadows Water Authority (TMWA); our engineers work within Nevada Division of Environmental Protection (NDEP) permitting and Nevada Division of Environmental Protection (NDEP) financing programs to deliver permit-ready, responsible-charge engineering.

Water and wastewater in Reno

Reno's water and wastewater service runs through Truckee Meadows Water Authority (TMWA). Truckee River operating agreement governs supply; industrial and residential growth drives treatment expansion.

Apex Grid engineers the systems behind that service: treatment-plant upgrades and expansions, lift stations and pump stations, collection-system rehabilitation, and the electrical, controls, and structural work that keeps plants operating. Designs are driven by the NPDES permit limits Nevada Division of Environmental Protection (NDEP) administers and positioned for the SRF financing Nevada Division of Environmental Protection (NDEP) administers — the engineering and the funding strategy developed together.

Drinking water and distribution in Reno

On the drinking water side, we design treatment improvements, AWWA-standard storage and distribution, pump stations, and hydraulic modeling that keeps pressure zones balanced and water age low. Statewide programs — lead service line replacement inventories and PFAS response where monitoring requires it — are reaching every Nevada municipality, and both are engineering-intensive, funding-eligible capital programs.

Streets, bridges, and stormwater in Reno

Municipal bridges and culverts in the Reno area follow the National Bridge Inspection Standards cycle with design coordinated to Nevada Department of Transportation (NDOT) and AASHTO LRFD. Funding and procurement for Reno projects

Nevada municipal projects compete for the same statewide funding: the Clean Water and Drinking Water SRF programs NDEP's Bureau of Water Pollution Control administers Nevada's Clean Water and Drinking Water SRF programs, tailoring loan terms — including interest-rate discounts and 20-to-30-year repayments — to borrower needs., with priority ranking through the annual Intended Use Plan. We prepare the engineering reports and environmental documentation that make projects competitive, and sequence design to funding windows.

Procurement runs on qualifications-based selection: the city issues an RFQ, scores firms on experience and personnel, and negotiates with the most qualified. Our qualifications lead with licensed engineers in Nevada and directly relevant municipal project experience — and our delivery keeps the schedule the RFQ promised.

  • SRF application engineering
  • Intended Use Plan positioning
  • RFQ-ready qualifications
  • Licensed engineers in Nevada
  • Fast, responsive delivery

Apex Grid Construction Delivery Branch

Apex Grid provides the engineering, code coordination, and permit-ready design. PCM extends the company into construction delivery, pre-construction planning, and field execution for commercial projects.

PCM keeps its established name and construction identity while operating as Apex Grid's construction delivery branch, giving owners, developers, and contracting teams one coordinated path from early scope and budget decisions through construction.

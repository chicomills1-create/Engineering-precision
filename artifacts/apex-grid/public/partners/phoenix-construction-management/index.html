Construction Delivery Alongside Engineering

Apex Grid provides the engineering, code coordination, and permit-ready design. PCM brings construction delivery, pre-construction planning, and field execution for commercial projects that need a capable general contractor at the table.

That combination gives owners, developers, and contracting teams one coordinated path from early scope and budget decisions through construction — while keeping each company's role clear.

Building Assessments

Independent engineering judgment for property transactions, renovations, and forensic analysis. We tell you exactly what you're buying or building on.

When Do You Need an Assessment?

Whether you are a developer acquiring a value-add commercial property, an owner dealing with mysterious structural settling, or a tenant planning a massive equipment upgrade—you need facts, not assumptions. Our assessments provide plain-English, actionable intelligence on the exact condition, capacity, and remaining lifespan of a building's core systems.

Assessment Deliverables

01

Condition Reports

Thorough visual inspections outlining immediate hazards, deferred maintenance, and anticipated capital expenditures.

02

MEP Life-Expectancy

Detailed inventory and analysis of aging HVAC, electrical gear, and plumbing, with cost-to-replace projections.

03

Seismic Risk (PML)

Probable Maximum Loss studies to satisfy lender requirements and evaluate the structural survivability of major seismic events.

04

Code Violation Reviews

Forensic analysis of unpermitted additions and changes of use to determine the feasibility of legalizing existing conditions.

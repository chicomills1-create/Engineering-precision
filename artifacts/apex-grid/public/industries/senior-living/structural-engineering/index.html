Structural Engineering for Senior Living Facilities

Senior living structural engineering addresses the unique building characteristics of residential care — accessible design requiring wider corridors and turning radii, medical equipment loads, and construction that minimizes disruption in occupied facilities.

What We Engineer

  • Assisted living and memory care structural systems
  • Skilled nursing facility structural design
  • Accessible design structural coordination
  • Medical equipment anchorage in senior care settings
  • Renovation structural engineering in occupied facilities
  • Structural addition design for campus expansion
  • Seismic design for senior living in high-hazard zones

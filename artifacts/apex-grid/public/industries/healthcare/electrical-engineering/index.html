Healthcare · Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering for Healthcare Facilities

Healthcare electrical engineering must comply with NFPA 99 essential electrical system requirements — life safety, critical, and equipment branches — with properly engineered transfer switching and generator capacity.

What We Engineer

  • Essential electrical system design — NFPA 99
  • Life safety, critical, and equipment branch engineering
  • Generator sizing for healthcare essential loads
  • Automatic transfer switch coordination
  • UPS systems for critical medical equipment
  • Electrical service sizing for large medical facilities
  • Imaging equipment dedicated circuits and power conditioning
  • Operating room isolated power panels
  • Ground fault protection requirements
  • Emergency lighting and exit sign systems

Quick Answers

What can electrical engineering include for Healthcare projects?
Typical scope topics include Essential electrical system design — NFPA 99, Life safety, critical, and equipment branch engineering, Generator sizing for healthcare essential loads. Final scope depends on the facility, existing conditions, jurisdiction, and requested deliverables.
What information is needed to scope this electrical engineering work?
Available drawings, site or facility conditions, intended use, project phase, jurisdiction, schedule, and required deliverables help establish a project-specific scope.
How are applicable codes and professional requirements confirmed?
The adopted codes, amendments, responsible professional, discipline, firm authorization, and authority-having-jurisdiction requirements must be verified for each project before coverage is confirmed.

Alabama

2015 IECC has been the state-referenced commercial energy baseline · 2A

Arizona

Varies by jurisdiction · 2B

Arkansas

Arkansas Energy Code for commercial buildings, based on the IECC / ASHRAE 90.1 (2021 IECC base in the current adoption) · 3A across most of the state, with 4A in the northern Ozark highlands

California

Title 24, Part 6 (2022 California Energy Code) · IECC 3B/3C along the coast, 4B/4C inland, up to 6B–7 in the Sierra

CO

Colorado

2021 IECC (state law now requires jurisdictions to adopt a recent energy code · 5B across the Front Range

CT

Connecticut

2021 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2019 (adopted as the Connecticut State Energy Code) · 5A across most of the state

DE

Delaware

2018 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2016 (Delaware energy code, adopted statewide by the Division of Energy & Climate) · 4A statewide — a humid, mixed-heating climate from the Wilmington/Piedmont north to the low, coastal Sussex County south

FL

Florida

Florida Building Code · 2A

GA

Georgia

Georgia State Minimum Standard Energy Code · 2A

HI

Hawaii

Hawaii Energy Conservation Code (based on the IECC, adopted through the State Building Code and county process) with ASHRAE 90.1 available as a referenced alternate · 1A

ID

Idaho

2018 IECC (Idaho-adopted, with state amendments) · 5B across the Treasure Valley

IL

Illinois

Illinois Energy Conservation Code · 5A across most of the state, transitioning to 4A in the far south

IN

Indiana

Indiana Energy Conservation Code based on ASHRAE 90.1-2007 / 2010-era IECC provisions with state amendments · 5A across most of the state, transitioning toward 4A in the far south near the Ohio River

IA

Iowa

Iowa Energy Code based on the 2012 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2010 with state amendments (adopted statewide) · 5A across most of the state

KS

Kansas

Varies by jurisdiction · 4A across most of the state

KY

Kentucky

Kentucky's commercial energy provisions follow the IECC / ASHRAE 90.1 edition incorporated into the Kentucky Building Code · 4A statewide

LA

Louisiana

2021 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2019 (adopted as part of the LSUCC commercial provisions) · 2A

ME

Maine

Maine commercial energy code within MUBEC (based on the IECC / ASHRAE 90.1 as adopted by BBCS) · 6A across most of the state

MD

Maryland

2021 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2019 (adopted within the MBPS as the Maryland energy code) · 4A across most of the state

MA

Massachusetts

Massachusetts commercial energy code (based on the IECC / ASHRAE 90.1 as adopted in 780 CMR 13/115) · 5A across most of the state

MI

Michigan

Michigan Uniform Energy Code based on the 2015 IECC (with ASHRAE 90.1-2013 as an alternate path) · 5A across the Lower Peninsula and 6A/7 across the Upper Peninsula

MN

Minnesota

Minnesota Commercial Energy Code based on ASHRAE 90.1-2016 / 2018 IECC with state amendments · 6A across most of the state

MS

Mississippi

IECC-based commercial energy provisions where adopted locally · 2A

MO

Missouri

Varies by jurisdiction · 4A across most of the state

MT

Montana

IECC-based Montana commercial energy code (recent cycles referencing the 2021 IECC with amendments) · 6B across much of the state

NE

Nebraska

Nebraska Energy Code based on the IECC (adopted statewide, historically tracking a 2018 IECC vintage) with ASHRAE 90.1 as an alternate path · 5A across the east and central state

NV

Nevada

2018 IECC (adopted by Clark County and the Southern Nevada jurisdictions · 3B

NH

New Hampshire

2018 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2016 (adopted as part of the New Hampshire State Building Code / State Energy Code) · 6A across most of the state

NJ

New Jersey

2021 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2019 (adopted as the NJ energy subcode) · 4A across the entire state — a humid, mixed climate stretching from the Atlantic shore to the northwest highlands, which run somewhat colder

NM

New Mexico

2021 IECC-based New Mexico Energy Conservation Code (statewide, with amendments) · 3B–4B across the desert south and central corridor

NY

New York

2020 Energy Conservation Construction Code of New York State (ECCCNYS), based on 2018 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2016 · 4A across the NYC metro, Long Island, and the Hudson Valley

NC

North Carolina

North Carolina Energy Conservation Code (a state-amended edition derived from the IECC with ASHRAE 90.1 as a referenced alternate for commercial buildings) · 3A across the Piedmont and coastal plain

ND

North Dakota

2018 IECC (as adopted within the State Building Code), with ASHRAE 90.1-2016 available as the referenced alternate for commercial buildings · 6A across most of the state, edging into 7

OH

Ohio

2021 IECC (as incorporated into the Ohio Building Code, with ASHRAE 90.1 as an alternate compliance path) · 5A

OK

Oklahoma

2018 IECC (adopted with the 2018 code package by the Uniform Building Code Commission) · 3A in the southeast, 3B/4B across the central and Panhandle regions

OR

Oregon

Oregon Energy Efficiency Specialty Code (OEESC), based on ASHRAE 90.1 / IECC with aggressive state amendments · 4C

PA

Pennsylvania

2018 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2016 (as adopted under the UCC) · 4A across the southeast

RI

Rhode Island

2018 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2016 (adopted as the Rhode Island energy code, SBC-8) · 5A statewide, with the coastal south and Narragansett Bay areas at the milder end of the cold-humid range

SC

South Carolina

2009 IECC as the statewide energy standard adopted by the Building Codes Council, with ASHRAE 90.1 available as a referenced alternate for commercial buildings · 3A across most of the state including Columbia, Charleston, and Greenville

SD

South Dakota

No statewide mandatory commercial energy code · 6A across most of the state, with 5A in the far southeast around Sioux Falls and cold high-elevation conditions in the Black Hills

TN

Tennessee

2018 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2016 as the statewide commercial energy baseline · 4A across most of the state, with 3A in the far southwest around Memphis

TX

Texas

2021 IECC is the state minimum for commercial construction (State Energy Conservation Office adoption) · 2A

UT

Utah

2021 IECC / ASHRAE 90.1-2019 (as amended by the state) · 5B across the Wasatch Front

VT

Vermont

Vermont Commercial Building Energy Standards (CBES), based on ASHRAE 90.1 / IECC and updated on a state cycle · 6A across most of the state

VA

Virginia

Virginia Energy Conservation Code (part of the 2021 USBC), based on 2021 IECC with amendments · 4A across most of the Commonwealth including the Tidewater, Piedmont, and Northern Virginia

WA

Washington

Washington State Energy Code (WSEC-C), 2021 edition · 4C

WV

West Virginia

2015 IECC (as adopted within the State Building Code) · 5A across most of the state, with 4A in the lower Ohio and Kanawha valleys and 6A along the highest Allegheny ridges

WI

Wisconsin

Wisconsin commercial energy code (SPS 363) referencing an IECC/ASHRAE 90.1 vintage adopted statewide · 6A across most of the state

WY

Wyoming

Varies by jurisdiction · 6B across much of the state

Plan Review Process

Provo accepts commercial permit applications through its online permitting services and coordinates building, planning, fire, and public-works review. Pre-application coordination is appropriate for larger projects; zoning, site-plan, utility, and right-of-way approvals can be separate from the building permit.

Local Amendments

Utah's construction code is statewide, while Provo's zoning, subdivision, hillside, fire, and development standards are local overlays. Confirm the adopted code transition and parcel-specific requirements with Provo before design is locked.

Utility Coordination

Rocky Mountain Power provides electric service and interconnection; Provo City operates municipal water, wastewater, and power-related utility services in its service area. Verify which Provo utility serves the parcel and resolve capacity and meter requirements early.

Provo FAQs

Which authority issues Provo commercial permits?
Provo Community Development and its Building Division coordinate commercial permitting, with planning, fire, and public-works reviews as applicable. Site zoning and utility approvals can be separate review tracks.
What energy code applies in Provo?
Utah's statewide construction code includes the 2021 IECC with Utah amendments for commercial work. Verify the current effective edition and Provo site requirements at application.
Why does a Provo geotechnical report matter?
Wasatch Front seismic demand, valley soils, snow, and foothill slope or drainage conditions vary materially by parcel. Those inputs can change foundations, lateral systems, grading, and retaining-wall design.

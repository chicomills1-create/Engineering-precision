California Design Environment

Building Code
2025 California Building Code (Title 24, Part 2), effective January 1, 2026
Energy Code
Title 24, Part 6 (2025 California Energy Code), effective January 1, 2026
Climate
IECC 3B/3C along the coast, 4B/4C inland, up to 6B–7 in the Sierra; California's own 16 climate zones drive Title 24
Key Markets
Los Angeles, San Francisco, San Diego, San Jose, Sacramento, Fresno

California enforces a statewide code through Title 24, published by the Building Standards Commission on a three-year cycle. The 2025 edition took effect statewide January 1, 2026, with local jurisdictions permitted to adopt more stringent amendments based on local conditions.

California remains the largest construction market in the country, driven by life sciences and biotech, data centers in the Central Valley, warehouse and logistics in the Inland Empire, and mandated housing and school modernization. Electrification and seismic-resilience mandates are reshaping both new construction and retrofit work.

PE-Stamped Drawings for Great Falls, Montana FAQs

Does a Great Falls PE seal replace industrial or fire review?
No. Community Development, fire, Public Works, utilities, and other authorities retain their own review responsibilities.
Can equipment loads be sealed without vendor information?
The PE should identify assumptions and limitations and obtain adequate data for independent judgment before relying on concentrated or dynamic loads.
Who decides if a Great Falls field change requires a permit revision?
The responsible PE evaluates its professional effect, while Great Falls or the applicable AHJ decides the permit consequence.

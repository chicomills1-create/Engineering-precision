Rochester Engineering FAQs

What code applies to a Rochester commercial building?
The city administers Minnesota's 2020 Building Code, based on the 2018 IBC with Minnesota amendments, and the 2020 Minnesota Energy Code.
Who provides Rochester utilities?
Rochester Public Utilities provides electric and water service; city systems provide sanitary sewer and stormwater. Confirm capacity, pressure, fire flow, and extensions for the parcel.
Why is snowmelt drainage important in Rochester?
Climate zone 6A cold, snow, frost, and freeze-thaw combine with Root River watershed drainage and glacial soils, so elevations, detention, erosion control, and outlets need early review.

MEP Engineering for Hartford Projects FAQs

What utility information is needed for MEP work in Hartford?
The answer depends on the MEP scope, existing conditions, current Connecticut requirements, and the information City of Hartford Development Services and Licenses & Inspections requests. A Hartford project should document those facts before the design is fixed.
How do ADU or residential-addition loads affect a MEP review?
City of Hartford Development Services and Licenses & Inspections may involve zoning, building, fire, floodplain, coastal, public-works, or utility reviewers. The project team should answer the cited correction with a coordinated revision and keep the response record.
What is a responsible response to City of Hartford Development Services and Licenses & Inspections MEP comments?
No. City of Hartford Development Services and Licenses & Inspections and the responsible Connecticut PE determine the required documents for the actual scope. Field conditions, code edition, occupancy, and project changes can add review.

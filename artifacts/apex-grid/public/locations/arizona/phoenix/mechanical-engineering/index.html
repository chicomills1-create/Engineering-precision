Phoenix Mechanical Engineering Questions

What does Mechanical Engineering in Phoenix include?
Phoenix mechanical engineering starts with the hardest climate problem in US commercial construction: cooling loads that run for six straight months, design temperatures above 110°F, and an urban heat island that erases the economizer credit other 2B cities still get.
Who reviews Phoenix engineering permit documents?
City of Phoenix Planning & Development Department (SHAPE PHX) is the listed authority having jurisdiction for this service area. The exact reviewer and submittal path should be confirmed for the project address before design begins.
Which building and energy codes apply in Phoenix?
The listed building-code basis is 2018 IBC / 2018 IMC with Phoenix amendments, and the listed energy-code basis is 2018 IECC with Phoenix amendments. Project-specific amendments and effective dates should be verified with the permitting authority.

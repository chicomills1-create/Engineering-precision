Local Amendments

North Carolina's statewide code supplies the technical baseline; Wilmington's land-development, tree, flood-damage-prevention, stormwater, and coastal site rules add local requirements. Coastal AEC and wind provisions become especially important near the Cape Fear River and Atlantic.

Utility Coordination

Wilmington FAQs

Which authority reviews Wilmington commercial building permits?
The City and New Hanover County coordinate commercial development and inspections, with county enforcement of the statewide building code and separate city or county site, zoning, stormwater, flood, and fire tracks. Confirm the parcel's assigned review path before submission.
What climate risks affect Wilmington design?
Atlantic hurricane wind, storm surge, coastal flooding, intense rainfall, salt air, and high groundwater all affect enclosure, structure, grading, equipment elevation, and corrosion protection. FEMA and local flood requirements are parcel-specific.
Who provides utilities in Wilmington?
Duke Energy Progress is the electric utility, and Cape Fear Public Utility Authority provides water and sewer in its service area. Commercial capacity, fire flow, service extensions, transformer needs, and connection timing must be confirmed for the site.

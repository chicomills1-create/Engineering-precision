What We Engineer

Mechanical engineering in building construction focuses on heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems — the equipment and ductwork that control temperature, humidity, ventilation, and air quality inside buildings. Commercial mechanical engineering is significantly more complex than residential HVAC because of code requirements for outdoor air, exhaust rates, energy efficiency, and specialty systems like commercial kitchen exhaust. Apex Grid mechanical engineers design systems that perform correctly, comply with energy codes, and pass plan check the first time.

  • Commercial HVAC system design and equipment selection
  • Commercial kitchen exhaust and make-up air engineering
  • Industrial ventilation and exhaust system design
  • Medical HVAC — pressure relationships, filtration, infection control
  • Energy code compliance — Title 24, ASHRAE 90.1, IECC
  • Rooftop HVAC unit sizing and duct distribution
  • Data center cooling engineering
  • Laboratory exhaust and fume hood engineering
  • HVAC replacement and upgrade engineering
  • Mechanical permit drawings and calculations
  • Plan check response for mechanical comments
  • Construction-phase mechanical RFI support

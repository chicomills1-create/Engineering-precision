How much does assisted living facility engineering cost?

By Jeremy Mills, CEO & Founder, Apex Grid Engineering — USAF Veteran. · Updated 2026-09-15

There is no responsible universal price for assisted living facility engineering. Fees follow the disciplines involved (MEP, structural, civil), the building's size and acuity level, new versus adaptive-reuse conditions, the authority's submittal requirements, and the deliverables — a useful proposal itemizes scope and assumptions instead of quoting a flat per-bed number.

What drives the engineering fee

Discipline count is the first driver: a full MEP plus structural plus civil package costs more than a single-discipline engagement. Acuity is the second — independent living is essentially multifamily engineering, while assisted living adds commercial kitchens, nurse call, wandering-prevention systems, and emergency power, and nursing adds health-care occupancy detailing. Existing buildings add survey, investigation, and as-built reconciliation. The authority's checklist — energy modeling, special inspections, third-party review — sets the documentation burden.

How senior living fees are usually structured

Most firms propose either a fixed fee against a defined scope or an hourly not-to-exceed for early feasibility work. Fixed fees work when the program, site, and AHJ requirements are known; hourly makes sense for entitlements support, peer review, or rescue work on a stalled permit. Either way, the proposal should name the deliverables, the code editions, the assumed existing-information quality, and how revisions are handled.

Where developers waste engineering budget

The expensive failure mode is late discovery: learning at plan check that the occupancy classification requires a different sprinkler standard, or that the kitchen needs a Type I hood the drawings never showed. A second waste is scope gaps between disciplines — the electrical engineer sizes a generator nobody coordinated with the fuel storage the civil engineer never drew. Integrated proposals with a single responsible coordinator cost less over the project than the cheapest per-discipline bids.

What else do project teams ask?

Is there a typical per-bed engineering cost for assisted living?
No responsible universal per-bed figure exists. Bed count correlates loosely with building size, but acuity level, kitchen scope, emergency power, site work, and jurisdiction move the fee far more than bed count alone.
Does memory care cost more to engineer than standard assisted living?
Usually yes. Memory care adds wandering-prevention and delayed-egress systems, higher staff-to-resident infrastructure, secure outdoor spaces, and often stricter AHJ scrutiny — each adds design and coordination scope.
Can I get a fixed fee before entitlements are complete?
A responsible fixed fee needs a defined program and site. Before that, firms typically offer a feasibility or entitlements-support phase on an hourly or capped basis, then convert to fixed fee once scope is known.
What should an engineering proposal include?
Deliverables by discipline, assumed code editions, what existing information the fee relies on, coordination responsibilities, revision allowances, exclusions, and schedule. Compare proposals on scope completeness, not headline price.

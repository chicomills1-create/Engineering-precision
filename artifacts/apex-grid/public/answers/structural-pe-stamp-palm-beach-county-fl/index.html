Structural PE Stamp in Palm Beach County, FL: What Gets Reviewed?

By Jeremy Mills, CEO & Founder, Apex Grid Engineering — USAF Veteran. · Updated 2026-09-15

A structural PE stamp in Palm Beach County certifies the gravity, lateral, and foundation systems after a Florida-licensed PE verifies the coastal wind design and calculations under responsible charge — with continuous load paths and flood-zone detailing getting the hardest scrutiny, submitted to the county or the municipality that owns the site.

I'm Jeremy Mills, CEO & Founder of Apex Grid Engineering and a U.S. Air Force veteran. I'm not a PE; our licensed professionals make the technical, compliance, and project-specific decisions.

Coastal wind and the load path

The structural review for Palm Beach County's coastal and near-coast sites centers on wind, and I verify it as a complete system. The wind design criteria get established for the site's actual exposure — distance from the coast changes the pressures — and the main wind-force-resisting system analysis gets checked against them. Component and cladding pressures get their own review pass: roofing, windows, doors, soffits, and wall cladding at the higher localized pressures the code assigns near the coast, because envelope failures are how hurricanes get inside buildings. The load path review runs link by link from roof to foundation: covering attachment, roof-to-wall connections, wall anchorage, shear wall holdowns, and foundation uplift resistance, each detailed with calculated capacities. Corrosion protection gets checked alongside the forces — stainless or properly coated connectors in the salt-air zone, because a perfectly sized connection that corrodes out is not a connection. The PE marks every gap before sealing, since Palm Beach County plan check — county or municipal — will find the gaps after.

Unincorporated county vs 39 municipalities

Palm Beach County's permitting fragmentation is among the highest in Florida: 39 municipal building departments plus the county's building division for unincorporated areas. Practically, this means the submittal mechanics — intake portal, checklist, fee schedule, correction conventions — vary by AHJ while the engineering standard does not. My groundwork on every project is a parcel-level jurisdiction confirmation before design begins, because the cost of guessing wrong is a full re-submittal into a different department's workflow. Once the AHJ is confirmed, the package is scoped to its intake standards: the seal block format, the calculation package organization, and any jurisdiction-specific forms. The review-and-seal engagement itself — responsible charge, verified calculations, marked revisions, then the seal — is constant. A complete first submittal, built for the right AHJ, with wind and flood criteria shown on the drawings and the geotech referenced on the foundation sheets, is the fastest path through any of the county's 40 doors.

Palm Beach County structural submittal checklist

A structural package is ready for the PE's review — and then for the AHJ — when these items are resolved. Coastal wind completeness and jurisdiction confirmation lead the list. • AHJ confirmed by parcel: Palm Beach County vs the specific municipality (39 possibilities) • Florida-licensed PE in responsible charge of the structural review • Coastal wind design criteria, exposure, and component/cladding pressures shown and verified • Continuous roof-to-foundation load path with every connection detailed for calculated forces • Corrosion protection for steel and connectors in the salt-air environment • Flood zone data, base flood elevation, and finished-floor elevation shown and coordinated • Geotechnical report with site-specific foundation recommendations; uplift resistance verified

What else do project teams ask?

How do I know which AHJ reviews my Palm Beach County project?
With 39 municipalities plus unincorporated county land, the parcel's jurisdiction must be confirmed — never assumed. West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Jupiter, Wellington, and the rest each run their own building departments with their own intake portals and checklists; the county's building division handles the unincorporated areas, which include significant coastal, corridor, and western communities. I verify by parcel at kickoff and confirm with the AHJ where boundaries are ambiguous. The engineering review is identical across jurisdictions, but the submittal package must be built for the department that will actually review it.
How does coastal exposure change the structural review?
Near-coast sites face higher wind design pressures and the most demanding exposure categories, which flows through the entire structural review: the wind analysis, the component and cladding pressures for the envelope, and the continuous load path with connections sized for the calculated uplift and shear. I also check corrosion protection for structural steel and connectors in the salt-air environment — a connection designed for the right force but built from the wrong material is a durability failure the review should catch. Flood and storm-surge provisions layer on where the maps require them. The sealed drawings show the coastal design criteria explicitly, because plan check in this county reads them closely.
What foundation conditions does Palm Beach County's ground create?
South Florida ground: sand and limestone with a high water table. The geotechnical report drives foundation type, bearing assumptions, and settlement expectations — shallow foundations where the ground bears well, deep foundations where it does not. The PE verifies the design against the actual report, and in flood zones the foundation review covers the flood-specific requirements: elevation, breakaway construction, or flood-resistant design where the maps govern. I also check uplift resistance coordination, because the wind loads pulling on the building have to be delivered into foundations designed to hold them. The sealed foundation sheets reference the report they were designed from.
What is in the structural calculations package?
The package documents the engineering behind the drawings: design criteria and load combinations, gravity member sizing, the wind analysis with component and cladding pressures, lateral analysis with drift checks, connection designs with calculated capacities, and foundation calculations tied to the geotechnical recommendations. It is organized so a county or municipal reviewer can trace any member on the drawings back to its calculation. I treat the calc package as part of the sealed deliverable — it is the evidence that the review behind the stamp actually happened, and reviewers in this market do open it.

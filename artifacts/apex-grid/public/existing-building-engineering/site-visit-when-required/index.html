When a Structural Engineer Needs a Site Visit

A structural engineer site visit may be required when existing conditions are complex, when contractor documentation is insufficient, when the AHJ requires direct observation, or when the engineering decision depends on directly observed conditions that cannot be reliably communicated through photos and measurements alone.

Scope of Services

  • Conditions that typically require a site visit — complex framing, inaccessible areas
  • When contractor documentation is sufficient to avoid a site visit
  • AHJ requirements for field observation on specific project types
  • What an engineer observes and documents during a site visit
  • Site visit scope — targeted vs. comprehensive observation
  • Coordination with contractor to expose framing before visit
  • Site visit report and engineering documentation
  • Timing site visits relative to construction and permit schedules

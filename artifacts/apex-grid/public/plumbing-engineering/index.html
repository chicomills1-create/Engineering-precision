Plumbing Engineering

Plumbing engineering designs the water, sanitary, and gas systems that supply and drain commercial buildings — from domestic water service sizing through fixture connections, sanitary drainage, grease interceptors, gas piping, and specialty systems for medical and laboratory facilities. Apex Grid plumbing engineers produce PE-stamped permit drawings for commercial, restaurant, medical, and multifamily projects.

What We Engineer

Commercial plumbing engineering covers the design of all piping systems that convey water, waste, and gas inside buildings: domestic hot and cold water supply, sanitary drainage and venting, storm drainage, gas piping, and specialty systems like medical gas and process piping. Commercial plumbing is significantly more complex than residential plumbing because of the variety of fixture types, occupancy loads, code requirements, and specialty systems involved. Apex Grid plumbing engineers coordinate with mechanical and electrical engineering to produce complete MEP permit packages.

  • Domestic water system sizing and design
  • Sanitary drainage and vent system design
  • Gas piping design for commercial cooking and heating
  • Grease interceptor sizing and design
  • Plumbing riser diagrams for multi-story buildings
  • Medical gas engineering — oxygen, vacuum, nitrous oxide
  • Water heater and domestic hot water system engineering
  • Plumbing fixture unit calculations
  • Storm drainage and rainwater system design
  • PE-stamped plumbing permit packages in 49 states

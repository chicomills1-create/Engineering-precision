Source and method

Source: https://www.census.gov/construction/bps/xls/cbsaannual_202599.xls. The corpus parses the “MSA Units Ann” sheet, keeps eligible Metro / Micro Code 2 records intersecting the reviewed eligibility footprint, and selects ranks 1 through 100 after ordering Total descending. Territories and records outside the reviewed footprint are excluded.

Representative city and state labels identify the metro record's primary label only. They do not claim an office, project, AHJ fact, county service area, or guaranteed coverage.

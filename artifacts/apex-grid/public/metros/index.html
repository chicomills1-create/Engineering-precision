Source and method

Source: https://www.census.gov/construction/bps/xls/cbsaannual_202599.xls. The corpus parses the “MSA Units Ann” sheet, keeps eligible Metro / Micro Code 2 records, and selects the first 50 records after ordering Total descending.

Representative city and state labels identify the metro record's primary label only. They do not claim an office, project, AHJ fact, county service area, or guaranteed coverage.

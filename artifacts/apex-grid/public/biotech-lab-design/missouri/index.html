BSL Lab Design in Missouri

BSL-1 through BSL-3 containment engineering for Missouri research programs: directional airflow, pressure cascades, non-recirculating exhaust, and the controls sequences that prove containment. Designed to BMBL and ASHRAE lab ventilation standards, sealed in-state.

Vivarium Engineering in Missouri

Animal research facilities engineered to the Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals: 10–15 air changes per hour, tight environmental control, cage-wash infrastructure, and ABSL containment where the program requires it.

Lab Ventilation & Fume Hoods in Missouri

Chemical fume hood exhaust per ANSI/AIHA Z9.5, VAV lab air systems that hold face velocity as sashes move, exhaust stacks designed against re-entrainment, and emergency purge modes engineered from day one.

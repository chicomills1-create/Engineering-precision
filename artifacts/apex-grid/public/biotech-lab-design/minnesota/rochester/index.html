Lab engineering scope for Rochester projects

What the engineering covers for research facilities in the Rochester market.

Containment & biosafety

BSL-1 through BSL-3 lab design per the BMBL: directional airflow, pressure cascades held by fast-acting VAV controls, non-recirculating exhaust, and sealed BSL-3 envelopes with redundant air systems. Rochester programs working with infectious agents get containment engineering that passes inspection the first time.

Ventilation & fume hoods

Chemical fume hoods per ANSI/AIHA Z9.5, laboratory exhaust stacks sized against re-entrainment, and emergency purge modes that clear a lab in minutes — designed into the air handlers and controls sequence from the start, not bolted on later.

Vivariums & animal research

Where the program includes animal research: 10–15 air changes per hour, temperature and humidity control, cage-wash steam and drainage, bedding handling, and ABSL-2/ABSL-3 containment layered on animal-care requirements.

Power, plumbing & structure

Dedicated lab panels and emergency power for freezers and critical ventilation; acid-waste, DI water, lab gases, and eyewash systems; floor loading and vibration criteria for heavy instruments — one coordinated MEP-plus-structural package.

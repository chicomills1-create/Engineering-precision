Frequently Asked Questions

How does the instant engineering estimator work?

The estimator uses the project type, approximate area, and location you provide to produce a preliminary, non-binding planning range. It is an early budgeting tool, not a proposal, construction estimate, permit-fee quote, or guarantee of final scope.

What affects MEP engineering fees?

MEP engineering effort depends on the building use and size, system complexity, existing-condition information, equipment selections, utility requirements, coordination needs, energy-code scope, permit deliverables, schedule, and the quality of the starting documents.

What affects structural engineering fees?

Structural fees depend on the structural system, loads, building configuration, existing conditions, available drawings, site observations, calculations, connection or foundation scope, coordination, and the documents required by the authority having jurisdiction.

How quickly will I receive the ballpark estimate?

The estimator displays a preliminary result after you provide the requested project information. A final proposal takes longer because the team must review the actual scope, available records, jurisdiction, disciplines, deliverables, and schedule.

Is the estimator result a final engineering proposal?

No. The result is a non-binding ballpark for early planning. Apex Grid confirms project-specific scope, professional responsibility, licensing, assumptions, exclusions, availability, and final fees before any engagement begins.

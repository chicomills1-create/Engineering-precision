Food Processing Plant Engineering in South Dakota

Beef, pork, and a dairy surge: South Dakota's processing economy runs from Sioux Falls packing to Milbank cheese.

By Jeremy Mills, CEO & Founder, Apex Grid Engineering — USAF Veteran

The South Dakota food industry

Cattle is South Dakota's top commodity at $3.9 billion, and food manufacturing concentration ranks among the nation's highest at 23.6 workers per 1,000.

Smithfield's Sioux Falls pork plant is one of the nation's largest, and Valley Queen Cheese completed a $200 million expansion in Milbank, now processing about 5 million pounds of milk daily.

The state ranks in the dairy top 10 nationally, with continued cheese and powder investment around the northeast milk shed.

Key production sectors: Cattle; Corn; Pork; Dairy. Agricultural regions: Sioux Falls corridor; James River Valley; Northeast dairy.

Engineering systems for meat and beef processing facilities

Northern plains winters: -25°F design temperatures, blizzards, heavy snow. Full winterization, backup power, and snow-load structures are non-negotiable; summers still bring heat and humidity.

  • USDA-FSIS-inspected process areas with sanitary design throughout
  • Ammonia refrigeration for blast chilling, coolers, and freezers
  • High-volume steam and hot-water systems for sanitation and rendering
  • NEMA 4X washdown electrical and sloped-to-drain epoxy floors
  • Blood, offal, and wastewater pretreatment coordination

South Dakota DANR permits discharge; USDA FSIS inspects the Sioux Falls pork plant and other meat facilities.

Fast-track delivery for South Dakota processors

Processors adding capacity are usually racing a contract start date or a seasonal harvest window. Our fast-track approach overlaps design packages with procurement and early site work, so refrigeration pads, utility mains, and structural steel move while final process details are still being coordinated — without losing the sanitary detailing that FDA and USDA reviewers check first.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is South Dakota's biggest food plant?

Smithfield's Sioux Falls pork processing plant — one of the largest in the country — plus the growing dairy cluster around Milbank. Both need USDA-level sanitary design and massive refrigeration.

Is South Dakota dairy still growing?

Yes — Valley Queen's $200 million Milbank expansion and the state's top-10 milk ranking signal continued investment in cheese and whey processing, all needing pasteurization, CIP, and industrial refrigeration engineering.

Angel Endres

Chief People Officer (CPO)

Angel Endres is a seasoned Human Resources executive with more than 15 years of experience in HR leadership, organizational development, employee relations, compliance, and workforce strategy. As Chief People Officer, Angel is responsible for strengthening the people, culture, and organizational infrastructure behind the company’s continued growth.

Throughout her career, Angel has built a reputation for combining the human side of leadership with the discipline required in highly regulated and technical environments. Her experience within the engineering sector has given her a strong understanding of the unique compliance, workforce, and professional standards that engineering organizations must navigate.

Angel has been recognized with professional awards for her work in HR and engineering compliance, reflecting her commitment to maintaining high standards while creating workplaces where employees are respected, supported, and positioned to succeed.

With extensive knowledge of SHRM principles and professional HR practices, Angel brings executive-level expertise in talent acquisition, employee development, performance management, workplace policy, regulatory compliance, organizational structure, conflict resolution, and leadership development.

What distinguishes Angel is her belief that Human Resources should be more than an administrative department—it should be a strategic part of the organization. She works closely with executive leadership to ensure that as the company grows, its people, policies, culture, and accountability grow with it.

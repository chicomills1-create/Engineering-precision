What I would change first

The biggest improvement would be turning Apex Grid from essentially a company website into an engineering search engine footprint.

Instead of trying to make the homepage rank for everything, build strong dedicated pages such as:

/structural-engineering/
/mep-engineering/
/civil-engineering/
/mechanical-engineering/
/electrical-engineering/
/plumbing-engineering/
/commercial-engineering/
/multifamily-engineering/
/residential-engineering/
/permit-engineering/

Then each page needs actual engineering substance—not 300 words of generic marketing copy.

For example, the Structural page should naturally discuss things prospective clients actually search for: structural calculations, foundation design, framing design, seismic design, existing-building modifications, additions, load-bearing wall modifications, structural inspections, permit drawings, PE-stamped plans, plan-check corrections, etc.

That gives Google dozens of signals about what Apex actually does rather than asking one homepage to establish all of that authority.

Location SEO could become huge.

If Apex serves multiple states/cities, I would build legitimate location/service pages around where you actually perform work.

For example:

Structural Engineer Phoenix AZ
MEP Engineering Phoenix AZ
Structural Engineer Los Angeles CA
MEP Engineering Dallas TX
Commercial Structural Engineering Arizona

But they cannot simply be identical pages with the city changed. Each should contain jurisdiction information, project types, codes/services, permitting context and relevant projects.

That's how I'd start attacking searches with actual purchasing intent:

structural engineer near me
structural engineer Phoenix
MEP engineer Phoenix
commercial MEP engineering
multifamily MEP engineer
PE stamped structural plans
structural engineering for architects
engineering firm for general contractors

Those visitors are substantially more valuable than somebody searching a broad informational engineering term.

There's another opportunity I really like for this company

Build pages specifically around who hires you.

For example:

Engineering for Architects
Engineering for General Contractors
Engineering for Developers
Engineering for Property Managers
Engineering for Multifamily Projects

That matches the actual buying process much better than simply saying “We provide engineering.”

Someone searching:

“MEP engineering firm for architects”

is probably considerably closer to hiring than someone searching:

“what is MEP engineering?”

Projects should become SEO assets too.

Instead of just having a generic portfolio, individual completed-project pages can establish expertise:

Multifamily MEP Engineering – Dallas, Texas
Residential Structural Engineering – Phoenix, Arizona
Commercial Tenant Improvement MEP – Los Angeles, California

Each case study can explain the project, engineering disciplines, challenge, solution, jurisdiction and deliverables without disclosing anything confidential.

That simultaneously helps Google, prospective clients, and AI search systems understand what Apex actually has experience doing.

Technical work I'd have the developer check immediately

I'd specifically tell whoever built the site to verify Google Search Console, XML sitemap submission, robots.txt, canonical URLs, unique title tags, unique meta descriptions, H1/H2 hierarchy, image alt text, image compression, Core Web Vitals, mobile performance, internal linking, redirects, Organization schema, ProfessionalService schema, Service schema, Breadcrumb schema and LocalBusiness schema where appropriate.

I'd also connect:

Google Search Console + GA4 + Bing Webmaster Tools.

Until Search Console is connected, we're partly flying blind. It will tell you which Apex pages Google has indexed, which it hasn't, what searches are already generating impressions, and whether Google sees technical problems.

The homepage itself

I'd make the title more commercially targeted depending on where the company operates.

Something closer to:

Structural, MEP & Civil Engineering Services | Apex Grid Engineering

And the H1 could be:

Full-Service Structural, MEP & Civil Engineering

Then immediately underneath:

Permit-ready engineering for architects, contractors, developers and property owners.

Then strong pathways:

Structural Engineering | MEP Engineering | Civil Engineering | Request a Proposal

That tells a human—and Google—exactly what the company is within seconds.

What SDVOSB Means for Your Agency
Engides' Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business certification gives contracting officers maximum flexibility — and gives agencies a path to meeting their small business goals while securing first-rate engineering.

SDVOSB Certified

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business

SDVOSB Set-Aside
Federal agencies are encouraged to award contracts to Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Businesses when two or more SDVOSBs can satisfy requirements at a fair price.

Sole-Source Awards
Under FAR 19.14, contracting officers may award sole-source contracts to a single SDVOSB up to $4.5M for services and $7.5M for construction without full competition.

VA Priority
The VA's VetFirst policy (38 U.S.C. § 8127) mandates that VA contracting officers give first priority to SDVOSBs — ahead of all other small business categories.

SAM.gov Registered
Engides is registered in SAM.gov and SBA's VetCert (formerly VA CVE) as a verified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, ready for federal procurement.

DoD Standards
Engineered to the Standard the Mission Demands
Military facilities operate under Unified Facilities Criteria — the DoD's comprehensive technical requirements for planning, design, construction, and operation. Engides designs to UFC standards across all disciplines, including antiterrorism force protection requirements that civilian firms often lack the experience to navigate.

We understand that military projects demand more than building-code compliance. They demand operational continuity, security integration, resilience against defined threat categories, and documentation standards that survive the full government QA process.

---
name: Free-first outreach research
description: Cost-control rule for finding evidence-backed outreach decision-makers.
---

Use official license registries, procurement records, and company team/contact/project pages to establish the firm, decision-maker, role, current fit, and any publicly listed business email before using paid enrichment. Use FindyMail only for a confirmed named person and confirmed company domain when an official source does not publish a direct address. Never infer an email from a naming pattern. One charged finder credit must produce one returned verified email; never spend finder credits on speculative names or domains.

**Why:** A public-source pilot found qualified decision-makers while consuming only three finder credits for four usable leads; one direct email came from an official firm opportunity page.

**How to apply:** Preserve the exact public URLs and evidence for every candidate, deduplicate and check suppression before promotion, and leave unresolved contacts out rather than spending credits indiscriminately or guessing. Complete the no-credit evidence review before enrichment, and rely on FindyMail's result-only finder billing so a no-result does not consume a finder credit.

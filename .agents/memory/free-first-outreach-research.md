---
name: Free-first outreach research
description: Cost-control rule for finding evidence-backed outreach decision-makers.
---

Use official license registries, procurement records, and company team/contact/project pages to establish the firm, decision-maker, role, current fit, and any publicly listed business email before using paid enrichment. Use FindyMail only for a confirmed named person and confirmed company domain when an official source does not publish a direct address. Never infer an email from a naming pattern.

**Why:** A public-source pilot found qualified decision-makers while consuming only three finder credits for four usable leads; one direct email came from an official firm opportunity page.

**How to apply:** Preserve the exact public URLs and evidence for every candidate, deduplicate and check suppression before promotion, and leave unresolved contacts out rather than spending credits indiscriminately or guessing.

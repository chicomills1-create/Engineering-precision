description: Evidence boundary for nationwide professional-service and location claims.
---

Do not publish a state-count licensure claim from the existence of state pages or an NCEES record. State pages describe markets and regulations, not proof that Apex Grid or a responsible professional is authorized there.

**Why:** Official national resources route verification to each licensing board; they do not establish one nationwide PE license. The reviewed official sources did not independently substantiate the prior 49-state claim, firm registrations, or named-license mappings.

**How to apply:** Before making a direct licensure, firm authorization, partner coverage, stamping, legal-name, or entity-status claim, retain the official detail record or filing tied to the responsible person or entity and review date. Otherwise describe the team as multi-state and confirm availability per project. Treat the Queen Creek address and phone as business-provided contact information, not proof of a local office or government-verified identity. ACC and Arizona BTR portals may block automated retrieval; a search without a captured matching detail record is a negative finding, not proof that no registration exists.

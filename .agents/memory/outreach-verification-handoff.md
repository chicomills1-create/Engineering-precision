---
name: Outreach verification handoff
description: Safety boundary for promoting researched companies into send-eligible outreach contacts.
---

Research discoveries are not send-eligible by themselves. Confirm a named current leader and exact role from visible content on the official company website. Use a visibly published same-domain role inbox when available; otherwise use FindyMail only with that confirmed name and official domain, and accept only a same-domain result.

**Why:** Generic research can find relevant companies without proving a contact identity or address. Promoting those rows directly would reintroduce inferred identities and email patterns.

**How to apply:** Keep discoveries in review/unverified/unknown until this evidence chain succeeds. A no-result stays unverified. Authentication, credit, suppression, duplicate-email, or duplicate-domain failures must block promotion rather than falling back.

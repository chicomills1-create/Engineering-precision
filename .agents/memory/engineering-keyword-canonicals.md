---
name: Engineering keyword canonicals
description: Safe expansion rules for high-intent engineering SEO and AEO pages.
---

Use one substantial canonical page for each distinct buyer problem or project stage. Put synonyms and long-tail phrases into visible sections and FAQs rather than creating near-duplicate landing pages. Consolidate overlapping urgency, permit, stamped-plan, assessment, and documentation searches into existing strong pages.

**Why:** Separate pages for semantically identical searches compete with each other and resemble doorway content. Speed, approval, licensure, availability, savings, and locality claims also create material trust risk when phrased as guarantees.

**How to apply:** Require useful intent-specific process, deliverables, boundaries, intake guidance, decision criteria, contextual inbound links, and visible factual FAQs. “Near me” content must distinguish organic search from actual offices, proximity, site-visit availability, and local-pack eligibility.

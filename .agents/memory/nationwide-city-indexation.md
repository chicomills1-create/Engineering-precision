---
name: Nationwide city indexation
description: Safe indexing policy for the nationwide programmatic city corpus.
---

Keep every historical and Census-derived city URL live and browseable, but use `noindex,follow` for directory-lite pages and derived architecture/general-contracting pages until they contain reviewed, genuinely local information. Submit curated CityData and explicitly retained locations through the location sitemaps.

**Why:** State-level copy with only a city-name or population substitution creates doorway/scaled-content risk. Deleting existing URLs creates a separate 404 and link-equity risk.

**How to apply:** Promote a city to `index,follow` only after adding verified AHJ, code, amendment, utility, climate, and market evidence. Keep sitemap inclusion synchronized with the page’s robots directive.

---
name: Nationwide city indexation
description: Safe indexing policy for the nationwide programmatic city corpus.
---

Keep every historical and Census-derived city URL live, but use `noindex,follow` for directory-lite pages and derived architecture/general-contracting pages until they contain reviewed, genuinely local information. State and vertical hubs must link only to reviewed/indexable cities; aliases redirect to one owner. Submit curated CityData and explicitly retained locations through the location sitemaps.

**Why:** State-level copy with only a city-name or population substitution creates doorway/scaled-content risk. Deleting existing URLs creates a separate 404 and link-equity risk.

**How to apply:** Promote a city to `index,follow` only after adding verified AHJ, code, amendment, utility, climate, and market evidence. Keep hub discovery and sitemap inclusion synchronized with robots, redirects, and exact self-canonicals. Do not place directory-wide internal-link grids to thousands of noindex cities; preserve live URLs without spending crawl budget advertising every thin page.

Before demoting or consolidating an established URL, check combined Search Console page-and-query evidence over roughly six months. Separate query-only and page-only totals cannot prove keyword retention. Any traffic-bearing URL outside the protected sitemap requires review and an explicit canonical destination or evidence-backed restoration.

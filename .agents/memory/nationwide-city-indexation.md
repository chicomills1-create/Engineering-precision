---
name: Nationwide city indexation
description: Safe indexing policy for the nationwide programmatic city corpus.
---

Nationwide means city-by-keyword coverage, not one anchor city per state. Each city should own one consolidated canonical hub covering the major discipline, facility, permit, assessment, renovation, architecture, construction, and buyer-intent clusters; do not create pages for wording variants.

**Why:** The business goal is to compete nationally for every major service intent in every U.S. city. Consolidated hubs preserve that breadth without multiplying synonyms into duplicate doorway pages.

**How to apply:** Index Census-verified incorporated places with a usable population signal through substantive consolidated hubs, synchronized sitemaps, and contextual state links. Keep smaller or incomplete records live as `noindex,follow` until official-data enrichment adds enough independent value. Promote those in waves rather than mass-indexing name-swapped pages.

Before demoting or consolidating an established URL, check combined Search Console page-and-query evidence over roughly six months. Separate query-only and page-only totals cannot prove keyword retention. Any traffic-bearing URL outside the protected sitemap requires review and an explicit canonical destination or evidence-backed restoration.
